# Reviewing a flagged resume

1. Read the finding type, where it was found, and the sample text shown as evidence.
2. Compare the hidden text with the visible resume. Ask whether it adds skills or claims that the visible resume does not support.
3. Check for an innocent cause, such as a template banner, a scanned-document text layer, or copy-and-paste characters.
4. Decide by the evidence, not the flag alone. Hidden instructions aimed at an AI screener, and large blocks of hidden keywords, are strong signs of manipulation. A few stray characters usually are not.
5. Judge the visible resume on its own merits. The visible text is what the candidate actually shows to a reader.
6. Record the decision and the reason in the audit log. Where the policy allows, give the candidate a chance to resubmit a clean file.

Principles: a flag supports human judgment and never replaces it. Treat all candidates the same way, and do not use hidden-text findings alone to reject someone automatically.
