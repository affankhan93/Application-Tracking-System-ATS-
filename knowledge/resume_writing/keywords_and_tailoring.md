# Keywords and tailoring a resume

## Why keywords matter
Recruiters and applicant tracking systems search for role terms such as skills, tools, and job titles. A resume that uses the same terms as the job description is easier to find and to match, as long as the claims are true.

## How to tailor a resume
1. Read the job description and list the required skills and tools.
2. Use the exact term the employer uses. Write "Power BI", not just "BI tools".
3. Write both the full name and the short form once, for example "Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)".
4. Place keywords in the skills section and also inside project or experience bullets, where they show real use.

## Do not stuff keywords
Repeating a keyword many times, hiding keywords in white or tiny text, or pasting a block of skills you do not have is treated as manipulation. It can lower the score and send the resume to manual review. Keywords should appear naturally, with context.

## When a required skill is missing
Do not claim a skill you do not have. Learn it and add a small project that uses it. Then list it with the evidence.

## Skills without evidence
A skill that appears only in the skills list is weak evidence. Make sure each major skill appears in at least one project or experience bullet that shows how it was used.
