# Common resume mistakes

- Spelling and grammar errors, especially in the job title, company names, or tool names.
- Inconsistent date formats, or dates that overlap without explanation.
- An unprofessional email address or missing contact links.
- A long paragraph summary or a generic career objective.
- Skill ratings drawn as bars or percentages (for example "Python 80%"). They are subjective and often unreadable by parsers.
- Tables, text boxes, and multiple columns that scramble the reading order.
- Listing every skill ever touched, including ones you cannot discuss.
- Task descriptions instead of achievements ("responsible for reports").
- No numbers, no results, and no links to projects.
- Irrelevant jobs given the same space as relevant ones.
- Using many fonts, colours, or sizes.
- A file named "resume.pdf". Use a clear name such as FirstName_LastName_Role.pdf.
- Outdated skills or tools listed first while current ones are buried.
