# Digital Marketing Executive: what recruiters look for

## Core skills (must have)
SEO, Google Ads, social media marketing, content marketing, email marketing, Google Analytics, campaign reporting, copywriting.

## Common tools
Google Analytics (GA4), Search Console, Meta Ads Manager, Mailchimp or HubSpot, Canva, SEMrush or Ahrefs.

## Nice to have
Marketing automation, A/B testing, conversion rate optimisation, basic design and video editing.

## Strong resume signals
Traffic growth, return on ad spend, click-through and conversion rates, and audience growth with time frames.

## Common gaps in fresher resumes
Follower counts only; no conversion or revenue metrics; no tool names; no budget size.

## Typical keywords
digital marketing, SEO, Google Ads, social media, content marketing, Google Analytics, email marketing, campaigns
