# HR Executive: what recruiters look for

## Core skills (must have)
Recruitment and talent acquisition (sourcing, screening, interviewing), onboarding, payroll basics, employee engagement, HRIS tools, labour law and statutory compliance (for India: PF, ESI, gratuity), communication.

## Common tools
An HRIS such as Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, or Zoho People; job portals; Excel; an applicant tracking system.

## Nice to have
HR analytics, performance management, learning and development, employer branding.

## Strong resume signals
Hiring numbers, time to hire, onboarding satisfaction, payroll accuracy, and the size of the team supported.

## Common gaps in fresher resumes
Vague phrases such as 'handled recruitment' with no numbers; no tool names; no compliance experience mentioned.

## Typical keywords
recruitment, talent acquisition, onboarding, payroll, HRIS, employee engagement, compliance, interviewing
