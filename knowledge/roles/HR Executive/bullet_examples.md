# HR Executive: before and after bullet examples

These examples show patterns. The numbers are samples. Candidates must use their own real figures and never copy a number they cannot support.

Weak: Handled recruitment.
Strong: Closed 45 hires in 12 months and reduced average time to hire from 40 to 28 days.

Weak: Did onboarding.
Strong: Ran onboarding for 120 new joiners with a 95% satisfaction score from feedback surveys.

Weak: Maintained records.
Strong: Digitised records for 300 employees in the HRIS, removing duplicates and cutting retrieval time to minutes.

Weak: Helped with payroll.
Strong: Processed monthly payroll for 300 employees with zero errors for 6 consecutive months.
