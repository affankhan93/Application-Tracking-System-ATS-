# Accountant: before and after bullet examples

These examples show patterns. The numbers are samples. Candidates must use their own real figures and never copy a number they cannot support.

Weak: Maintained books of accounts.
Strong: Maintained books for 3 entities in Tally and reconciled 500+ vendor accounts monthly, clearing 98% of mismatches within 5 days.

Weak: Handled GST.
Strong: Filed GST returns for 3 entities on time for 24 consecutive months with no penalties.

Weak: Prepared reports.
Strong: Cut month-end closing from 8 days to 5 days by automating reconciliations in Excel.

Weak: Assisted with audit.
Strong: Prepared schedules for the annual audit of 3 entities; the audit closed with no major findings.
