# Accountant: what recruiters look for

## Core skills (must have)
Bookkeeping, Tally, Excel (formulas, pivot tables), GST and TDS or the local tax equivalent, bank and vendor reconciliation, financial reporting, audit support, an ERP such as SAP.

## Common tools
Tally Prime, Excel, SAP or another ERP, banking portals, GST portal.

## Nice to have
Ind AS or IFRS, Power BI, budgeting and forecasting.

## Strong resume signals
Number of entities or accounts handled, filing timeliness, month-end closing time, reconciliation accuracy.

## Common gaps in fresher resumes
No software names; no compliance details; no volumes or timelines; only duties listed.

## Typical keywords
accounting, bookkeeping, Tally, GST, reconciliation, financial reporting, auditing, taxation, Excel
